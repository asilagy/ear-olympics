# The resource region in the host simulator: the cast, the title art, Dogtor and the intro music are kept OUTSIDE the app's image (fm1.toml
# resources, app.h APP_RES_BASE), read through plat_res. The simulator has them compiled in; "res 0" is an FM-1 that does not hold them.
#   1. with the region: the movie plays, and a backup and restore of the saved settings round-trips (the resource write is the restore's transfer)
#   2. without it (a flash that was not given the resources): no movie, no music, no art, a plain notice; nothing crashes; the games still play
#   3. with it again: the art is back at once
# States: 0 ready, 9 movie, 10 select.
wait 600
expect res 1
expect state 9
expect smp 0
# a restore is refused while the movie plays (STOP FIRST), as the resource write would be
tap HOME
wait 50
tap PLAY
wait 550
tap HOME
wait 50
expect game 1
expect state 0
turn SELECT 2
wait 30
expect root 9
tap SAVE
wait 30
expect dirty 0
backup out:eo_res.bak
turn SELECT 3
wait 30
expect root 12
tap SAVE
wait 30
restore out:eo_res.bak
wait 100
expect root 9
expect dirty 0
# everything reloaded as at power-on: the movie again, with its art and music
expect state 9
expect res 1
shot res_restored_movie.png
# ---- 2. no resource region
res 0
reboot
wait 300
expect res 0
expect smp 0
expect state 10
shot res_missing_select.png
turn PRESET 1
wait 30
shot res_missing_select_dogtor.png
# the music never starts, and any key does nothing odd
wait 3000
expect smp 0
# a game still plays without its pictures: Dogtor's round runs, HOME goes back
tap PLAY
wait 600
expect game 2
tap HOME
wait 100
turn PRESET -1
wait 30
turn PRESET -1
wait 30
tap PLAY
wait 600
tap HOME
wait 100
tap HOME
wait 100
expect state 10
# ---- 3. the region is there again: the art is back at once, and a restart plays the movie
res 1
wait 100
expect res 1
turn PRESET 1
wait 30
shot res_back_select_dogtor.png
reboot
wait 300
expect res 1
expect state 9
wait 2200
expect smp 1
